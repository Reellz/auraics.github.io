Why Aura?

Holistic Excellence

Blending academic rigour with deep spiritual formation.

At AICS we cultivate holistic excellence through involvement — every student builds Godly character through daily devotions, scripture memorization, and service opportunities.

Nurturing Community

Fostering a Christ-centered, homely environment in partnership with families.

We provide a homely learning environment where parents are equipped to help shape a Godly generation, in true partnership rather than at arm's length.

Future-Ready

Cultivating the creative and technical skills for tomorrow.

From digital literacy to creative problem-solving, students graduate ready to serve confidently as Godly citizens with a global vision.
